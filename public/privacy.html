Privacy Policy

SkyOracle

Last updated: October 17, 2026

1. Overview

SkyOracle (developed by Lapsed Academics) is designed with privacy as a foundational principle. The app provides stargazing forecasts, celestial ephemeris, twilight schedules, and naked-eye planetary visibility guides.

2. Information Collection and Use

No Personal Data Collection: We do not require registration, login accounts, email addresses, or personal identity information. We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

Location Information: If you explicitly grant location access (via GPS or IP-based network lookup), geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude) are used on your device exclusively to calculate:

  • Local solar twilights (sunset, civil, nautical, and astronomical dusk/dawn).
  • Rise and set times for the Moon and planets relative to your horizon.
  • Hourly cloud cover forecasts from the open weather API.

Your coordinates are stored locally in your device's browser/application storage for your convenience and are never transmitted to or stored on any proprietary servers.

3. Third-Party Services

The application queries the following public, privacy-friendly endpoints to retrieve open telemetry:

  • Open-Meteo API: Cloud cover forecasts and optional city name search (Open-Meteo Privacy Policy). Open-Meteo does not require API keys or log user identities.
  • ipwho.is: Used solely as a seamless fallback if browser geolocation fails (IPWhoIs Privacy Policy).

4. Data Storage and Offline Operation

Astronomical math (solar twilights, planetary ephemeris, moon phase and age) is executed 100% offline within your device. Recent locations you search or save remain stored locally on your device.
