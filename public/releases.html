Version 1.0.18 (Build 5)

November 2026

Internationalization (5 languages), Android 15 edge-to-edge support, and header layout stabilization.

🌐 Full Internationalization (i18n) Engine

  • 5-Language Lineup: Full native translations for English (EN), Spanish (ES), Portuguese (PT), German (DE), and French (FR). Japanese (JA) is scheduled for the upcoming release.
  • Zero Dependencies & 100% Offline: Ultra-lightweight dictionary engine (~1.2 kB gzip) with dynamic parameter interpolation and persistent device locale detection.
  • Comprehensive Translation: Localized observing forecasts, hourly cloud tracks, dew point alerts, twilight milestones, moon phases, planetary visibility statuses, and interactive 10° starfield HUD overlays.

📱 Android 15 Edge-to-Edge Compatibility

  • Zero Screen Cutout Overlap: Integrated CSS safe-area insets (env(safe-area-inset-top), env(safe-area-inset-bottom)) for smooth rendering around device cutouts, camera notches, and gesture navigation bars.
  • Native Backward Compatibility: Implemented androidx.activity.EdgeToEdge.enable(this) in LauncherActivity.java and migrated away from deprecated window display APIs.

⚡ Header Usability & Release Notes Link

  • 1-Tap Release Notes: Tapping the version badge (v1.0.18) in the header opens this changelog and release history page directly.
  • Stable Navigation Toolbar: Restructured top navigation layout to ensure utility and location buttons remain stably anchored across all supported languages and screen widths.

Version 1.0.17 (Build 4)

November 2026

Major usability, starfield typography, interactive onboarding, and lunar/solar ephemeris release.

🔤 Starfield Typography & Night-Vision Contrast

  • High-Contrast Halos: Added thick 3.5px contrast halos (rgba(2, 6, 17, 0.95)) behind all star labels, ensuring constellation stick-lines and background stars never cut through text.
  • Legibility Upgrade: Increased star and companion body text from 9.5px regular to bold 11.5px, and centered target labels to bold 12px for instant readability under dark sky conditions and high-DPI mobile screens.
  • Horizon & Facing Heading: Upgraded compass azimuth headings to bold 11px monospace with protective halos for arm's length observation.

💡 Interactive 4-Slide Feature Tour

  • Guided Carousel: Added a clean 4-slide interactive modal walking through the 10° eyepiece, Natural Perspective level horizon, time scrubber, and celestial ruler.
  • Discovery Callouts: Added a permanent 💡 Tour button in the top navigation and discovery callout banners above the target table.

🌙 Moon & ☀️ Sun Centered Telescopic Starfields

  • True-to-Scale Discs: Centering on the Moon (0.5° scale) or Sun (0.53° scale) renders physically scaled bodies matching the field of view.
  • Real-Time Star Occultation: The Moon renders an opaque earthshine disc that physically occults background stars as it drifts at 0.5°/hour across the celestial sphere.
  • Solar Safety Guidance: Dedicated Solar Observation Hazard banner advising against unshielded optics when centered on the Sun.

🕒 Fluid 2-Hour Time Scrubber

  • Smooth 60fps Scrubbing: Drag ±1 hour with 36-second precision or jump with ±2h outer buttons.
  • 1-Tap Horizon Locks: Instant 🌇 Set and 🌅 Rise buttons lock objects right on the green horizon line (0.0° altitude) for rising and setting simulations.

📏 Tap-to-Inspect & Celestial Ruler

  • Interactive Inspection: Tap any star or companion planet in the field to draw a live distance ruler displaying angular separation (degrees & arcmin) and position angle bearing.

⚡ 100% Offline Resilience

  • Service worker caching shell and local astronomical algorithms (NOAA solar algorithms + Jean Meeus lunar/planetary series) operate with zero network requirement.
